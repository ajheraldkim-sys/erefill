# E-Refill Use Case Diagram

This folder includes an editable SVG use case diagram modeled after the provided sample layout.

Open the diagram here:

[use_case_diagram.svg](./use_case_diagram.svg)

Actors included:

- Customer
- Station Owner
- Rider

Main relationships included:

- Customer can browse nearby water stations, choose a refill type, place orders, and proceed to checkout.
- Refill ordering includes water type, quantity, container size, delivery address, and payment method.
- Checkout includes a refill summary, Cash on Delivery or PayMongo payment, and live order tracking.
- Station Owner can manage refill pricing, station availability, orders, riders, reports, and payouts.
- Rider can view assigned deliveries, navigate to pickup and customer addresses, upload proof, and complete delivery.
